Diagnosis before editing
The grammatical core had been framed as useful almost exclusively for distribution. That restriction recurred in the abstract, introduction, §2.1, §3.2 and conclusion; §4 stressed the failed inference from unusual phonology to grammatical membership while neglecting the supported forward expectation. It made the pluralism look like a division into disciplinary compartments.

Changes
The manuscript now treats grammatical interjectionhood as supporting a wider, defeasible profile of meaning, use and realization. Separate intonational packaging is characteristic; unusual segmental phonology is a recurring possibility, not a universal or measured prevalence. Potts's existing expressive generalizations receive positive weight across standalone and prenominal damn. Section 4 distinguishes the forward association, possible reverse evidential value, and an insufficient reverse membership test. The discussion and conclusion explain why partial associations make classifications mutually informative without rendering them interchangeable.
