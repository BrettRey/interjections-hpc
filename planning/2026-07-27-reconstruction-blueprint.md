# Reconstruction blueprint: English interjection categories
## Decision
Reconstruct the existing paper; do not start from a blank repository or merely patch the current argument.

The existing manuscript contains valuable literature work, examples, historical cases, figures, appendices, and one strong conditional residue result. Starting over would recreate these assets and risk losing their evidential qualifications. But the title–abstract–introduction–projectibility–conclusion chain has to be newly written because it assigns projective force to bare _interjection_, treats the three-way overlap as an inferential source, and claims empirical payoffs the analyses do not demonstrate.

The right unit of action is therefore an **architectural reconstruction**:

- preserve and regrade the empirical modules;

- replace the argumentative spine;

- make the three categories and their field-neutral bearer explicit near the beginning;

- make **route-specific residue** the strongest present empirical result: later sign-types retain traces of how they entered or left a category, such as taboo-related phonological deformation, a source construction's complement pattern, or source-interjection meaning in a verbal derivative;

- treat held-out forward projectibility as a declared research hypothesis on the present evidence;

- run a CABNC feasibility pilot now, then decide from prespecified gates whether a full held-out study can join this paper or should become a separate empirical paper.


This is an iteration of the approved programme, not a pivot away from it.
## Governing architecture
1. English inquiry has reason to distinguish `INTERJECTION_syn`, `INTERJECTION_sem`, and `INTERJECTION_prag`.

2. All three classify a conventionalized linguistic sign-type. Contrasts in grammar, meaning, or interaction may individuate sign-types; grammatical distribution has no priority.

3. The subscripts name principal projection families, not pure property provenance.

4. Bare _interjection_ is a cover term for canonical threefold cases. Their overlap has no independent category standing and supplies no fourth projective payoff. It also causes confusion when readers assume that the bare term must name one of the three principal projection families.

5. Category labels are analysts' representations. Participant cue use is a separate empirical claim.

6. The primary bearers of warrant are explicit projective declarations.

7. The present version may motivate forward declarations and directly support some retrodictive residue claims. It may claim demonstrated held-out forward prediction only if the new CABNC study passes its pilot gates, is preregistered, and succeeds under its declared defeat conditions.


Two compact analogies belong near the first presentation of the categories. WtWHS confirms that **proper noun/proper name** is the cleanest syntax–semantics case: two legitimate categories cross-cut the same sign-types because they preserve different distinctions and support different projections, while both draw on mixed properties. The manuscript's existing **interrogative/question** contrast supplies the syntax–interaction case. Together they triangulate the three domains without inventing a questionable directive-semantics category. WtWHS also offers semantic/morphosyntactic countability and definiteness/deitality, but the first largely repeats the syntax–semantics lesson and the second would import unfamiliar terminology.
## Iteration checkpoint
### What we learned
- Two independent architecture reviews accepted the three-category ontology once the bearer was made field-neutral.

- All three Round 1 audits found pervasive fourth-category leakage and admission–target leakage in the current manuscript.

- The empirical audit found no direct held-out test of forward projectibility, no statistical test of category-wide co-occurrence, no demonstration of temporal stability, and no category-wide establishment of causal-network order.

- The strongest direct positive result is semantic residue in selected included verbal derivatives; the strongest negative result is the failed _fie_ ordering; the strongest historical case is Gehweiler's documented _Jesus!_ to _gee!_ pathway.

- Local Mistral and GLM models did not clear the threshold for architecture or argument review. They may still be useful for mechanical checks.

### Assumptions that changed
- **Changed:** the existing analyses cannot support projectibility as an empirically demonstrated central payoff. Only a new, independently coded held-out study could change that status.

- **Changed:** GloWbE cannot carry a claim about boundary decomposition or item-type-specific variety structure.

- **Changed:** the causal network must be presented as evidentially graded; most links are candidates or reconstructions.

- **Preserved:** the empirical and scholarly assets are substantial enough that reconstruction is better than a blank-page restart.

- **Preserved:** the three-category ontology is the paper's distinctive theoretical contribution.

### Decision
**Iterate.** Rebuild the argument around the approved architecture and a stricter empirical hierarchy.

## Revised claim hierarchy
### Central theoretical claims
1. _Interjection_ is not a single classification applied uniformly across grammar, semantics, and interaction. English inquiry tracks three real, overlapping categories with different extensions and principal projective families.

2. The three categories classify field-neutral conventionalized sign-types. Syntax has no prior ontological standing.

3. Canonical interjections satisfy all three classifications, but the overlap is not another category.

4. Recruitment histories and source-specific residues motivate causal and historical relations among the categories and their properties.

5. Those relations generate explicit, defeasible forward projectibility hypotheses. Their held-out predictive success remains to be tested directly unless the proposed CABNC study passes its feasibility and confirmatory gates.

### Principal present empirical claim
Among four selected overt derivative families, every included VP-head token retained source-interjection semantics. This is conditional evidence for path-dependent exit residue, not evidence that interjections generally gain morphology or syntax.
### Supporting empirical claims
- Gehweiler documents parts of an invocative _Jesus!_ to _gee!_ pathway and tabooistic deformation.

- The exact _damn_ pathway is reconstructive; complement retention is compatible with verbal-source residue.

- The preregistered ordered-erosion prediction for _fie_ received low posterior support; its node-specific dates were not distinguished.

- The tagged corpus inventory turns over; this does not itself demonstrate persistence of a category position.

- GloWbE contains overall country-section variation and strong concentration for some measured items; it does not establish item-type-specific category-boundary structure.

- Item-specific descriptions of _lah_, _haba_, and _yaar_ support different grammatical and regional profiles; their role in maintenance or social indexing remains hypothetical.

### Prospective claims
- Partial evidence for one category may support predictions about an unobserved property or environment within that category.

- Independently established membership in one category may support defeasible expectations about another.

- A causal-network representation may generalize better than flat features, prototype distance, or constructional inheritance under partial observation.

### Claims retired from this paper
- Bare _interjection_ is a fourth or higher-order category.

- Membership in bare _interjection_ has independent projective force.

- The current analyses demonstrate held-out forward projectibility.

- The diagnostic matrix shows statistically reliable co-occurrence.

- The profile is demonstrated to be stable and causally ordered category-wide.

- Any maintenance mechanism or corrective control has been established.

- GloWbE demonstrates decomposition of category gradience by variety.

- Inventory turnover demonstrates persistence of a projectible category position.

- Interjections generally gain morphology or syntax on exit.

- _Fie_'s three profiles declined simultaneously or at equal dates.

## CABNC held-out study feasibility

### Verdict

A direct held-out study is feasible now, but not by reanalysing the project's existing datasets. The morphology sample has four preselected derivative families and an all-positive retained-semantics outcome; the _fie_ analysis has one sign-type; and GloWbE lacks sequential recipient outcomes. None can test whether an independently assigned source category predicts an unobserved interactional target in a new sign-type.

The best route is the [Conversation Analysis British National Corpus](https://talkbank.org/ca/access/CABNC.html): approximately 4.2 million words from 1,436 natural conversations, with transcripts, audio, and turn/word timing. Preliminary discovery counts came from an obsolete duplicate subtree and are retired. The reproducible census uses the pinned `cabnc_talkbank_chat` subtree at commit `0a28a11e168e312d1b9ad406a3352f31c13b86a2`; its 1,860 files are transcript segments, not automatically independent conversations.

### Primary declaration

- **Bearer:** conventionalized linguistic sign-type, represented by eligible tokens nested in form family, speaker, and conversation.

- **Observed source:** declaration-specific `INTERJECTION_syn` and/or `INTERJECTION_sem`, assigned from independent dossiers after withholding the exact target and its close proxies. Full-profile membership remains mixed-domain and is recorded separately.

- **Withheld target:** the recipient's next-sequence treatment, coded as fitted uptake/response, continuer or backchannel while the prior speaker retains the floor, or no uptake/same-speaker continuation. Timing and overlap are secondary outcomes.

- **Primary relations:** `syn/sem → recipient-treatment property`. The interactional target is not identical to `INTERJECTION_prag` membership, and the study does not by itself establish the full three-category ontology.

- **Holdout:** the entire orthographic/phonological form family, so every use of a form such as _oh_ is confined to one fold. Primary predictions integrate over new form, speaker, and conversation effects; conditional and cross-group analyses are secondary.

- **Blinding:** source coders do not see the following turn or post-offset timing; target coders do not see source labels, category hypotheses, or form-level predictions.

### Comparison and evaluation

Compare six representations under leave-one-form-family-out evaluation:

1. target base rate;
2. immediate token-local cues;
3. the eligible mixed-domain dossier diagnostics as an unstructured vector;
4. neighbouring classifications such as supplement, expressive, or response-token status;
5. declaration-specific `INTERJECTION_syn`, `INTERJECTION_sem`, and their predeclared interaction;
6. `syn`-only and `sem`-only edge ablations.

Use a predeclared multilevel categorical model with form-family, sign-type, speaker, and conversation structure as supported by the census. State and prior-predictively calibrate model-appropriate priors; verify implementation through simulation-based calibration; and criticize fitted models with posterior-predictive checks. Evaluate macro-averaged held-out log score or ELPD, Brier score, and family-conditional calibration. Declare negligible and useful regions before confirmatory outcomes are linked, and report full family-level score differences and design-operating curves rather than one threshold.

### Pilot gates

Proceed to a preregistered confirmatory study only if the blinded pilot establishes:

- enough positive, discordant, and control families to identify the declared edges: provisionally 24 across the four cells, or at least six families and roughly 150 events on each side of a predeclared single-edge contrast;
- target-coding agreement of Krippendorff's alpha or weighted kappa at or above .70;
- source-coding agreement at or above .80;
- no source-target leakage under an independent audit;
- enough within- and between-family target variation for simulations across plausible null, small, and useful effects to support a discriminating test.

The likely confirmatory scale is roughly 1,500–2,500 coded events and 120–180 annotation hours. CABNC is an existing recorded corpus and requires no REB review; its audio access, storage, and use must comply with the AudioBNC research-use terms and BNC User Licence. CHILDES is not a same-estimand substitute: HSLLD mealtimes may serve as a later caregiver–child replication, with Gleason dinners as a linked-audio audit. If CABNC fails its construct or yield gates, the direct-test fallback is the independently normed semantic-repetition experiment.

### Decision rule for this paper

- **Pilot fails a gate:** keep forward projectibility explicitly prospective and treat a redesigned study as separate work.

- **Pilot passes:** preregister and run the full CABNC study before finalizing the manuscript's evidential spine.

- **Confirmatory study succeeds under its declared margin and calibration criteria:** integrate it as the direct test in Section 5.

- **Confirmatory study fails:** report the failure and demote or abandon the affected projective edge; do not split off an inconvenient null result.

### 5. What each category may let us predict
- Rewrite from scratch around the declaration matrices above.

- State the strongest defeat condition: if structured category/network representations do not improve held-out generalization or residue alignment beyond matched rivals, demote the causal/projective claims.

### 7. Conclusion
- Reassert three legitimate categories and no independent overlap category.

- Name conditional derivative residue as the strongest direct result.

- Preserve the _fie_ failure and heterogeneous pathway statuses.

- End with a precise projective research programme, not a completed predictive victory.
