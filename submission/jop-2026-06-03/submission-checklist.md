# JoP Submission Packet Checklist

Prepared: June 3, 2026

Journal: Journal of Pragmatics

Article type: Full-length article

Required files prepared:

- Manuscript without author details: manuscript-without-author-details.pdf
- Manuscript source copy: manuscript-without-author-details.tex
- Cover letter: cover-letter.docx
- Declaration of competing interests: declaration-of-competing-interests.docx
- Highlights: highlights.docx
- Author biography: author-biography.docx
- Title page with author details: title-page-with-author-details.docx

Checks:

- Blind review is enabled in the manuscript source.
- PDF author metadata is blank in the blind manuscript.
- Abstract is under 250 words.
- Keywords are limited to 7.
- Highlights contain 5 bullet points.
- Each highlight is 85 characters or fewer, including spaces.
- Generative AI use is declared in the manuscript.
