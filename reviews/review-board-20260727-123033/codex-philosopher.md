## 1. One-sentence summary

The paper argues that English interjections constitute a genuine lexical category because a historically recruited, causally ordered property cluster licenses defeasible predictions about forms’ prosodic, syntactic, semantic, and interactional behaviour, without requiring an essence or homeostatic corrective control.

## 2. Strengths

- The non-homeostatic positioning is coherent and unusually disciplined. The paper distinguishes the projective declaration from its warrant ([line 219](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:219)), stability from network order ([line 232](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:232)), and ordinary stabilizers from corrective control ([line 239](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:239)). Its strongest version is therefore not “these mechanisms constitute an HPC,” but “these links explain why the profile supports induction.” That is a coherent Khalidi-style use of causal structure without Boyd-style homeostasis.

- Projectibility is genuinely central rather than decorative. The manuscript specifies observed and predicted properties, unit, population, and the prohibition on merely predicting an admission criterion ([line 219](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:219)); confronts the circular formulation explicitly ([line 1994](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:1994)); and usefully distinguishes analysts’ category-driven projection from participants’ cue-driven inference ([line 2098](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2098)). That gives general linguists enough conceptual orientation without presupposing natural-kind theory.

- The apparatus is connected to empirical risk. The link-status table distinguishes documented, reconstructive, candidate, and hypothetical relations ([line 1238](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:1238)), while the failed preregistered prediction for *fie* is reported as a real boundary on the account ([line 1828](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:1828)). This makes the causal-network proposal more than retrospective relabelling.

## 3. Weaknesses

- The three-node solution threatens to dissolve the category whose reality the paper is meant to establish. The introduction says “interjection” covers forms occupying all three nodes ([line 335](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:335)); §3.4 then says the individual nodes are projectible for different purposes and that the traditional label merely names their “zone of overlap” ([line 1218](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:1218)); §5 again defines the bare category extension as their intersection ([line 1983](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:1983)). But an intersection of three projectible nodes is not automatically itself a causal or projectible node. The paper must either demonstrate an incremental predictive payoff belonging to the intersection over and above its components, or revise the thesis toward three overlapping field-relative categories. The causal diagram should also distinguish causal arrows from constitutive or classificatory relations.

- “Projectibility in Goodman’s sense” is currently too strong ([line 2098](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2098)). Most examples involve completing the profile of one item from other properties already observed on that item ([line 1994](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:1994)); moreover, the paper ultimately says the category label is only an analyst’s bookkeeping device and that the cues and coupling perform the inferential work ([line 2107](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2107)). A defensible post-Goodman account needs the two-stage induction stated explicitly: observations across previous cases warrant a lawlike conditional, which then supports prediction about an incompletely observed new case. It should also say what causal robustness contributes beyond correlation and whether the label improves prediction conditional on the cues. Otherwise, call this “cross-property prediction” rather than specifically Goodmanian projectibility. The attribution to Goodman (1955) requires verification against the original; I have not independently read that source for this review.

- The sharp-but-fluid position is possible but presently unnecessary and under-argued. Determinate diagnostics do not by themselves yield determinate category membership, especially when the diagnostic matrix itself admits “partial or debated” values ([line 841](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:841)). The paper assumes a sharp but non-locatable edge ([line 776](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:776)) and then concedes that none of its arguments requires that assumption ([line 788](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:788)). For this readership, the cleaner solution is to retain form-, environment-, speaker-, and time-indexed diagnostic determinacy while dropping or clearly quarantining metaphysical sharpness. Similarly, “only corrective control” licenses *homeostatic* ([line 241](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:241)) should be presented as the author’s deliberately strict terminological policy, not as an uncontested interpretation of Boyd, unless verified directly against Boyd (1999), which I have not independently checked here.

## 4. Key question

What novel outcome is predicted better by assigning a form to the intersection called *interjection*, over and above the cues that justify that assignment and the predictions already supplied by the three component nodes?

## 5. Verdict

**Revise & Resubmit** — the non-homeostatic causal-network programme is coherent and promising, but the manuscript has not yet shown that the traditional category itself, rather than its component nodes or observed cues, supplies the claimed Goodmanian inductive payoff.
