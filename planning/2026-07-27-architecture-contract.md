# Architecture contract: three interjection categories
## Status and purpose
This contract fixes the ontology, inferential burden, and evidential ceiling of the reconstructed paper before any prose in `main.tex` is changed. It is a constraint on the rewrite, not prose intended for submission. If a proposed sentence conflicts with this contract, the sentence changes unless the contract is first reconsidered explicitly.
## Central thesis
English linguistic inquiry has reason to recognize three overlapping but non-identical interjection categories. The three classifications track different positions or cuts in one mixed network of prosodic, morphological, syntactic, semantic, interactional, conventional, and historical relations. Each classification is distinguished by the family of projections for which it is principally useful, not by being constituted solely from properties belonging to one disciplinary domain.

The paper's central question is therefore not whether one privileged syntactic category generates two derivative profiles. It is whether the three classifications are independently motivated, whether each supports non-trivial projections, and whether the patterned overlap and divergence among them are explained by causal and historical relations rather than stipulated in a diagram.
## Common bearer
The common bearer of the three classifications is a **conventionalized linguistic sign-type**. A sign-type pairs an expressional form with a conventional profile that may include grammatical realization, meaning contribution, and interactional affordance. A stable contrast in any of those dimensions may distinguish one sign-type from another; grammatical distribution has no priority in fixing the grain.

Thus verbal _damn_ in _I damn you_, standalone _damn!_, complement-taking _damn these mosquitoes!_, and prenominal _damn_ in _the damn car_ instantiate distinguishable sign-types. So may two tokens with the same narrow distribution but different conventional meaning contributions or interactional affordances. Tokens supply evidence about a sign-type; the three category classifications apply to that sign-type.

This common bearer licenses set-theoretic overlap among the three categories. It also prevents the Venn diagram from treating a distribution, a meaning contribution, and an interactional action as though they were three objects of the same type.

The interaction-facing category therefore classifies the sign-type **by virtue of the interactional actions it conventionally affords**. It does not classify the action token itself. Likewise, the semantics-facing category classifies the sign-type by virtue of its conventional meaning contribution, rather than treating that contribution as the bearer.

Every comparative table row must represent one conventionalized sign-type. A formally identical expression with distinguishable conventional profiles must receive separate rows when those profiles diverge in any relevant dimension. In particular, a single undifferentiated row for polyfunctional _oh_ is incompatible with this grain of analysis.
## The three categories
### Grammar-facing interjection
`INTERJECTION_syn` is the classification whose principal projective family concerns grammatical distribution and packaging. Its admission evidence is primarily morphosyntactic and constructional: characteristic supplement function or standalone distribution, morphological invariance in the relevant use, and severe restrictions on ordinary argument structure and integration.

This is a lexical category in the ordinary grammatical sense. Calling it grammar-facing does not make it ontologically privileged over the other two categories.
### Semantics-facing interjection
`INTERJECTION_sem` is the classification whose principal projective family concerns conventional meaning contribution. Its admission evidence concerns a use-conditional or expressive contribution: independence from at-issue content, perspective dependence, nondisplaceability, and related diagnostics. It may include syntactically integrated constructions, such as prenominal _damn_, when their conventional meaning warrants the classification.

This is a genuine linguistic category even though it is not a morphosyntactic word class. Its properties need not be exclusively semantic; the classification may be identified and warranted through relations to form, syntax, use, and history.
### Pragmatics-facing interjection
`INTERJECTION_prag` is the classification whose principal projective family concerns conventional interactional action and sequential organization. Its admission evidence must identify a pragmatics-facing category without building every proposed sequential consequence into membership. Minimal response, stance, repair, continuer, and change-of-state uses are candidate regions, but the reconstruction must fix a principled admission rule and keep its projection targets independent.

This is a genuine linguistic category even though it is not a morphosyntactic word class. Its properties need not be exclusively pragmatic; its interactional affordances may depend on prosodic, semantic, syntactic, and conventional relations.
## Meaning of the subscripts
The subscripts index the **principal projection family** associated with a category cut. They do not mean:

- that every admission property comes from the subscripted discipline;

- that the category is real only relative to a researcher's interests;

- that practitioners in one field are forbidden from using another cut;

- that the three cuts must be equally rich or structurally symmetrical.


Field or inquiry relativity fixes the question and scope. It does not fix the answer. Evidence and worldly organization determine whether a declared projection succeeds.
## Status of bare _interjection_
Bare _interjection_ is a conventional cover term for canonical sign-types that satisfy all three classifications. The overlap has **no independent category standing** in this paper. It is not a fourth node, a higher-order natural kind, or an additional source of projectibility.

Nothing prevents the analyst from referring compactly to canonical interjections. Joint membership also makes all three node-specific projective families available. But this is inherited from the component classifications: the conjunction does no further explanatory or inferential work.

Consequently:

- claims about category standing must specify which interjection category is at issue;

- claims about what "membership" predicts must identify the source classification;

- the bare term may describe canonical cases or the traditional domain, but may not silently alternate among the three classifications;

- the Venn overlap illustrates canonical convergence rather than discovering a fourth category.

## Category, label, and participant inference
Category labels are scientific representations of structure, not causal forces. Speakers and hearers may exploit cues and learned dependencies without representing any of the three labels. Analysts use the labels to compress, communicate, and test families of generalizations.

There is therefore no contradiction between saying that a category label is an analyst's bookkeeping device and saying that known category membership licenses an analyst's expectation. The intended relation is epistemic: membership summarizes an independently structured pattern. It need not add information after a complete diagnostic vector is already known, cause the predicted behaviour, or occur in a participant's psychology.

Claims that participants actually project from partial cues require their own psycholinguistic or interactional warrant. They may not be inferred merely from the usefulness of the analyst's classification.
## Projective declarations
The primary bearers of warrant are projective claims, not the three categories in the abstract. Each declaration must specify source, outcome, bearer, population, conditions, transformations, timescale, tolerance, evidential standard, and failure conditions.
### Category individuation versus declaration-specific evidence
The full family of diagnostics that individuates a category must be distinguished from the evidence actually observed for a particular bearer in a particular projective declaration. A category need not be redefined around a deliberately impoverished pair of cues merely to create a target. Instead, a declaration states which subset of diagnostics or environments has been observed in the case at hand and asks what this warrants about a genuinely unobserved property or environment.

Accordingly, supplement function may belong to the general individuation profile of `INTERJECTION_syn` and still serve as a target in a declaration whose case-level source evidence did not include supplement behaviour. What is forbidden is counting the same observed supplement behaviour first as the reason a sign-type was assigned in that declaration and then as its predicted outcome. The same discipline applies to all three categories.

The reconstruction will distinguish two families.
### Within-category projections
These move from admission evidence to a target property of the same classification that was deliberately withheld from admission.

- For `INTERJECTION_sem`, immediacy and intensifying rather than redundant repetition are candidate targets because they need not be used to assign the category.

- For `INTERJECTION_prag`, next-turn treatment, recipient uptake, return-of-floor consequences, and sequential flexibility are candidate targets only when they are neither constitutive of the action used to identify the source category nor observed in assigning the particular case. Backchanneling may appear on the source or target side of a declaration, never both. A stronger design predicts behaviour in an unobserved sequential environment from classification established elsewhere.

- For `INTERJECTION_syn`, a restricted set of observed distributions may license expectations about untested frames, coordination possibilities, complementation, or supplement behaviour. Ordinary distributional consequences may remain near-entailments or consequences of component features. The paper must not present them as distinctive causal-network successes unless the case-level target was genuinely unobserved and the inference reaches beyond what the source observation already supplied.

### Cross-category projections
These move from independently assigned membership in one category to an expectation about another category or its withheld properties. Examples include whether grammar-facing classification supports an expectation of use-conditional meaning, or whether semantics-facing classification supports an expectation about interactional availability.

The six possible directed cross-category relations need not all hold, and need not be symmetrical. Any retained edge must have admission evidence that excludes its target, an explicit population and condition, an evidential warrant, and a failure rule.

The paper will not ask whether a category label adds unique variance after the complete deterministic vector used to compute that label is already in a model. A legitimate stronger empirical test compares ways of generalizing under partial or noisy observation, ideally on held-out lexical constructions or varieties.
## Present evidential ceiling
The current manuscript contains several kinds of evidence with different inferential roles.

- The history of _gee_ supplies documented evidence for parts of an invocative recruitment pathway.

- Complement and phonological residues motivate retrodictive links between present behaviour and recruitment history, with the _damn_ pathway partly reconstructive.

- The morphology study supports semantic residue among selected derivative forms in included VP-head uses. It does not show that interjections generally gain morphology or syntax.

- The _fie_ study is a genuine failed prediction: it does not support the preregistered order of node erosion.

- GloWbE supports additional country-level variation in overall item frequencies. It does not establish item-type-specific country effects or decomposition of category gradience.

- Inventory counts illustrate turnover in a tagged inventory. They do not by themselves establish persistence of a projectible category position.

- Routine entrenchment, prosodic–syntactic coupling, regional/indexical maintenance, and several directions in the proposed network remain differently graded hypotheses or candidates.


The strongest currently defensible world-side conclusion is that some recruitment pathways and source-specific residues are documented, while parts of a broader causal ordering are motivated but not established as a category-wide network. Stability and causal order must be argued separately. Effective maintenance is not established. Corrective control and homeostasis are not claimed.

Unless the revision adds direct evidence, it may present the three-category projective architecture as a specified and partially warranted hypothesis. It may not say that held-out forward predictive success has been demonstrated.
## Principal empirical and theoretical contribution
The theoretical contribution is the three-category analysis: grammar, semantics, and interaction can make different legitimate cuts through a mixed linguistic network, with no syntactic presumption that only the grammatical cut is a category. Boundary cases matter because they reveal the cuts' non-identity and the particular links that have decoupled.

The strongest present empirical contribution is path-dependent residue: different recruitment and exit routes leave different traces. This result can support parts of a causal-network account without establishing every proposed edge or the forward projectibility of every category.

The paper succeeds if it shows:

1. that each interjection category is independently individuated rather than manufactured around examples;

2. that each retained category figures in at least one non-trivial, sufficiently warranted projective claim, or is candidly marked as prospective;

3. that causal and historical relations explain patterned convergence and divergence better than a flat list, prototype, feature bundle, or constructional inventory alone;

4. that the evidential status of every link is visible and preserved in the conclusion.

## Rival accounts and defeat conditions
The architecture is defeated or requires demotion if:

- a proposed category has no principled admission rule beyond relabelling a broader established class;

- all of its apparent projections repeat its admission criteria;

- the three classifications cannot apply coherently to a common bearer;

- apparent field relativity reduces to researcher preference rather than differently scoped true generalizations;

- residue patterns do not align with independently warranted pathway histories;

- a flat feature, prototype, or constructional account captures the same successes and failures without the proposed causal ordering;

- failed declared projections are recoverable only by excluding the failures after the fact.


Failure of one category's projective claims does not automatically abolish the other two. Failure of the forward projectibility thesis does not by itself show that `INTERJECTION_syn` is not a grammatical lexical category. The claims must be demoted separately.
## Claims explicitly not made
The reconstructed paper does not claim:

- that the overlap of the three categories is independently a category;

- that syntax has prior or superior ontological standing;

- that the three categories contain properties from only one linguistic domain;

- that speakers or hearers represent the analyst's category labels;

- that a label contributes information conditional on its complete diagnostic basis;

- that all proposed causal links have equal evidential support;

- that stability entails causal order, or that either entails maintenance;

- that the profile is homeostatic or under corrective control;

- that country variation establishes item-type-specific boundary structure;

- that inventory turnover alone establishes persistence of a projectible category;

- that the account generalizes unchanged across languages.

## Terminological discipline for the rewrite
- Prefer a subscripted category whenever a membership or projective claim is made.

- Use bare _interjection_ for the traditional domain or canonical threefold cases only.

- Use _category complex_ for the connected analytical domain only if it is explicitly denied independent category standing.

- Use _profile_ for a worldly combination of properties, not as a demotion of the semantic or interactional categories.

- Use _supports_, _motivates_, _is compatible with_, _documents_, and _demonstrates_ according to the claim–evidence ledger rather than for rhetorical variation.

- Never use _pragmatic projectibility_ as a standing label; the domain of a projection is a result to be established.

## Architecture-gate review and dispositions
Two independent frontier-model reviews reached the same verdict: **approve with required revisions**. Gemini 3.1 Pro and Claude Opus 4.6 Thinking both accepted the three-category ontology in principle and identified the same principal implementation risks in the current manuscript: fourth-category leakage from bare _interjection_, admission–target leakage in the grammar- and interaction-facing declarations, occasional drift from field relativity into interest relativity, and inconsistent grain of analysis for polyfunctional forms such as _oh_.

The contract now incorporates those findings as follows:

- the common bearer is fixed as a conventionalized linguistic sign-type whose grain may be determined by contrasts in grammar, meaning, or interaction, and the semantic and interactional classifications apply to that bearer by virtue of its meaning contribution and interactional affordances;

- bare _interjection_ is explicitly denied independent category standing and independent projective force;

- field relativity fixes the scope of a claim, while evidence fixes its truth and warrant;

- category individuation is distinguished from the subset of evidence observed in a particular projective declaration;

- backchannel behaviour, supplement function, and analogous diagnostics may occur on either side of a declaration but may not do double duty in the same case;

- comparative tables must separate formally identical but conventionally distinct uses.


One reviewer proposed thinning the general admission criteria for `INTERJECTION_syn` to prosodic set-off and non-inflection so that supplement behaviour could remain a prediction. That recommendation is accepted only in its underlying concern, not literally. Redefining a category around an artificially impoverished cue pair would manufacture projectibility. The adopted solution keeps a fully motivated category profile while requiring each declaration to identify the evidence actually observed in the case and a genuinely unobserved target.

The reviews also leave one major burden for the paper rather than for this contract: it must show that the three cuts do more than rename established neighbouring classifications such as the CGEL word class, expressives, and response or liminal signs. That burden belongs in the rival-accounts analysis and in the explicit projective declarations.

Two local models were piloted on the same audit. Mistral Small 24B reproduced the ontology but did not reliably distinguish cautions in the contract from defects in the manuscript. GLM 4.7 Flash spent its run restating the inputs without reaching the requested audit. Neither clears the critical-path threshold. Local models may still be used for low-cost mechanical checks after the argument is settled, but not as architecture judges or lead prose revisers.
## Questions for independent review
1. Does the common-bearer proposal make the three classifications coherent without forcing them to be categories of the same linguistic type?

2. Does the contract preserve the ontological parity of non-syntactic categories while using _lexical category_ in its ordinary grammatical sense?

3. Is bare _interjection_ genuinely prevented from acquiring fourth-category standing elsewhere in the contract?

4. Are the proposed within-category and cross-category projections non-trivial in principle, or do they still leak admission criteria into targets?

5. Does the evidential ceiling understate or overstate anything supported by the present manuscript?

6. What is the strongest objection that would survive a rewrite faithful to this contract?
