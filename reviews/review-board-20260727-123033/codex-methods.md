### 1. One-sentence summary

The paper argues that English interjections constitute a genuine lexical category because observing part of their prosodic, syntactic, semantic, and interactional profile licenses defeasible predictions about unobserved properties, with corpus studies probing conditioning, pathway residue, and failure.

### 2. Strengths

- The empirical claims are unusually well bounded. Section 6.1 calls the GloWbE analysis a “proof-of-concept probe,” identifies the CVs as primary, and explicitly separates these tests from corrective control ([main.tex:2300](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2300), [main.tex:2378](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2378)). The conclusion likewise distinguishes established stability and network order from merely candidate grounds ([main.tex:2562](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2562)).

- The `ha` audit is used well within its proper scope. Coding all twenty sections exposes country-varying precision, demonstrates that the CV depends materially on whether laughter counts, and propagates the resulting uncertainty through sensitivity refits ([main.tex:2399](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2399), [main.tex:2753](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2753)). The explicit distinction between precision and recall is exactly right ([main.tex:2619](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2619)).

- The preregistered COHA work reports an informative failure rather than rescuing the hypothesis: the predicted ordering for `fie` receives posterior probability 0.13 and is declared unobserved ([main.tex:2947](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2947)). Blinded time coding and reported intra-coder reliability further strengthen that result ([main.tex:2893](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2893)).

### 3. Weaknesses

- The GloWbE estimand does not match the strongest prose. The paper predicts that apparent category gradience should “decompose” under conditioning and concludes that country “recovers structure at every level” ([main.tex:2324](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2324), [main.tex:2366](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2366)). But m1 adds only a common country random intercept; it tests whether overall token rates differ by corpus section, not whether item types or category boundaries behave differently by country. The relevant varying-slope model, m3, is exploratory and cannot be ranked reliably ([main.tex:2694](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2694), [main.tex:2710](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2710)). Moreover, observation-level LOO predicts a missing cell for an already-observed item and country; it does not test generalization to a new item or variety. Recast the result as “GloWbE section predicts overall rates,” or fit an adequately supported item-by-country/type interaction and use leave-one-item- or leave-one-country-out validation appropriate to the intended projection. Given ΔELPD = 17.9 with SE = 13.3, “directionally favourable but uncertain” is warranted; “outperforms” or “preferred” is too firm ([main.tex:2370](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2370)).

- The validity disclosures are sufficient to warn readers but also undercut the variety interpretation. Core counts are tag-restricted, whereas `haba` and `yaar` are raw strings; recall can be zero, regional raw strings contain non-English and proper-name tokens, and “country” belongs to a webpage rather than a speaker ([main.tex:2627](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2627), [main.tex:2652](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2652)). Correcting `ha` precision shows that this one contaminant does not drive m1, but it validates neither the other seventeen outcomes nor recall. The manuscript should report actual audit sample sizes, sampling protocol, coding reliability, and sensitivity models restricted to consistently measured, manually validated items. Otherwise “country” must be interpreted as corpus-section composition—including genre and internet ecology—not linguistic variety.

- The COHA conclusions need narrower wording. In the morphology study, overt derivatives are selected and the success criterion then includes `verbal_syntax = 1`; thus syntactic integration is substantially built into the sampling frame, while the real empirical result is preservation of broadly coded source semantics among 10–17 early tokens per family ([main.tex:2774](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2774), [main.tex:2823](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2823)). Report that conditional result rather than a general morphology-gain trajectory. For `fie`, Pr(predicted ordering)=0.13 supports rejection of that ordering, but overlapping marginal ED50 intervals do not establish “simultaneous decline” ([main.tex:2947](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2947), [main.tex:2955](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/main.tex:2955)). Establish simultaneity with a joint model and a declared equivalence margin for pairwise ED50 differences; also report predictive checks for the monotonic logistic form given the acknowledged late rebounds.

### 4. Key question

What precisely is the held-out prediction that “country conditioning” improves—an unseen token for a known item and corpus section, a new item, or a new English variety—and can that prediction be demonstrated on consistently hand-validated outcomes rather than tagger-dependent counts?

### 5. Verdict

**Revise & Resubmit** — the theoretical argument is strong and the transparency exemplary, but the principal GloWbE model does not test the decomposition claim attributed to it, and both the measurement regime and COHA interpretations require substantial recalibration.
