## Rules

- Read the actual file. Cite sections and line numbers or quote.
- Source grounding: if you cite a paper or finding, flag it as needing
  verification unless you have read it. Do not invent citations.
- Rapoport's Rules: show you understand the argument, and state its strongest
  version, before criticizing.
- Be specific. "Section 3 is weak" is useless; name the passage and the fix.
- Don't pull punches. The point is to find problems before a referee does.
- Do not edit any file. Do not read other reviewers' output.
