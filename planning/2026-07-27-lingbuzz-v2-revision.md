# LingBuzz v2 revision
## Decision
Revise the existing manuscript now and release the result as a new version of the existing LingBuzz record. Do not start from scratch, create a new LingBuzz record, or wait for the CABNC study.

This revision will be a self-standing theoretical and empirical paper, not a protocol paper. It will distinguish what the present evidence establishes from the direct projectibility claims that the CABNC study will test later.
## Provisional title
**English Interjections Across Grammar, Meaning, and Interaction: Field-Relative Categories and Path Dependence**

The title can be revisited after the new abstract and conclusion exist, but the current title cannot remain because _What Membership Lets Us Predict_ presents held-out predictive success as an achieved result.
## Fixed architecture
- `INTERJECTION_syn`, `INTERJECTION_sem`, and `INTERJECTION_prag` are coequal, inquiry-relative linguistic categories cutting through one mixed network.

- The subscript identifies the inquiry for which the classification has its principal projective use. It does not identify a domain-pure bundle of properties.

- The common bearer is a conventionalized linguistic sign-type.

- Canonical cases belong to all three categories.

- Their intersection is not a fourth category and has no independent standing or projective force.

- Bare _interjection_ is a conventional cover term for the traditional domain or for canonical cases; substantive membership claims identify the relevant subscripted category.

- Category labels summarize structure for analysts. Claims about participant inference require separate evidence.

## Evidential ceiling
The revised paper may claim that:

- the three fields make principled, non-identical category cuts;

- documented and reconstructive recruitment pathways converge on recurrent profiles;

- source-specific and exit residues support path dependence;

- the _gee_ history documents parts of the invocative pathway;

- selected derivative forms retain source-interjection semantics in included VP-head uses;

- the preregistered _fie_ ordering failed;

- GloWbE shows additional country-level variation in overall item frequencies;

- parts of a broader causal ordering are supported or motivated;

- the resulting architecture specifies non-trivial projectibility hypotheses.


It may not claim that:

- the three-way overlap is independently a category;

- held-out forward projectibility has been demonstrated;

- participant cue-driven prediction has been established;

- inventory turnover establishes persistence of a projectible category position;

- GloWbE establishes item-type-specific country effects or decomposes category boundaries;

- the proposed network order, maintenance processes, or corrective control have been established as a whole.

## Manuscript changes
1. **Title, abstract, and introduction.** Reframe the central result around three field-relative categories and path dependence. State projectibility as an explicit, partially warranted hypothesis awaiting direct held-out evaluation.

2. **Category architecture.** Define the common bearer; explain the proper-noun/proper-name analogy; state what the subscripts mean; remove fourth-category leakage; and revise the Venn-diagram discussion so the overlap shows canonical convergence only.

3. **Causal structure.** Preserve the evidence-status table and make the same grading govern the surrounding prose. Distinguish documented pathways, reconstructive histories, ordering links, stabilizer candidates, and maintenance hypotheses.

4. **Projectibility section.** Retain the source–target discipline and the strongest candidate targets: semantic immediacy and repeatability, recipient uptake, next-turn treatment, return of the floor, and genuinely unobserved grammatical environments. Present these as declarations generated by the account, not as measured successes. Add a compact statement of the planned held-out test without turning the section into a CABNC protocol.

5. **Empirical corrections.** Narrow the GloWbE result; use cleaned or explicitly upper-bound regional counts; describe morphology as semantic residue conditional on derivative VP-head uses; report the failed _fie_ ordering without inferring simultaneous decline from overlapping marginal posteriors; treat the _damn_ pathway as reconstructive; qualify complement-taking _damn_ consistently; and describe COHA only as inventory turnover.

6. **Remove dispensable material.** Delete the sharp-but-unlocatable boundary excursus and other claims that do no work. Reduce repetition where the property survey, causal section, projectibility section, and discussion currently restate the same examples.

7. **Conclusion.** End with the strongest warranted result: three legitimate category cuts, patterned convergence and divergence, path-dependent residue, partial support for causal ordering, and an explicit direct-test agenda.
