AUTHOR'S THEORETICAL STEERING
The point of the paper is the general account of inquiry-relative classification, not an empirical theory of interjections. INTJ_syn, INTJ_sem, INTJ_prag are three worked cases in an OPEN FAMILY. x denotes an inquiry, not a disciplinary department. One inquiry can use several classifications; one grouping can serve several inquiries. All candidates face equal assessment standards but can have unequal evidential support. Candidate standing, supported classification, comparative merit, and causal explanation are distinct.
For each proposal specify bearer, grouping basis, intended contribution (distinction/inference/explanation), scope/community/time, and alternatives. No extra requirement that new candidates be disjoint, statistically independent, sharply bounded, uniquely mechanistic, or already predictive when the existing three are merely prospective. Distinct inferential work isn't statistical independence.
Phonological organization and ancestry should be the chief developed extensions, economical because existing paper already relies on them. Distinguish a phonological stratum of sign-types from prosodic classes of situated realizations. Distinguish present signs classified by independently established ancestry from classes of historical changes/processes. Membership intersections require common bearers; other bearers need explicit realization or source-result relations, not extra columns in the same matrix. Ameka primary/secondary is synchronic other-use status, not un-derived/derived history; gee documented Jesus route makes this clear; unknown etymology is not a shared origin. Separate historical source, mechanism (clipping/reanalysis), transmission/borrowing setting, subsequent trajectory. Descendants of one innovation aren't independent histories.
Broader social-regulation and processing categories can intersect traditional interjection domain without being interjection-only. Distinguish category existence, its intersection with traditional domain, and usefulness of INTJ_x naming. A category can be best called expressives/formulaic expressions. Historical explanation need not retain extra predictive effect after controlling every present consequence (mediators); frequency can be part of processing mechanism, not invariably nuisance. Avoid universal machine-learning benchmark as price of category recognition. Other candidates (morphology, semiotics, development, multimodal/graphic, annotation) need compact bearer/contribution table/discussion, not encyclopedic literature survey.
Intersection of initial three left open: derivability from full component vector doesn't prove no representational utility under sparse/noisy data. No automatic fourth privileged category either.
