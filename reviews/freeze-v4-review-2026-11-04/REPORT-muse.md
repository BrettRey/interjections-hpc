## 3. Where I disagree with decisions already taken

First, P08 = N by stated contrast (§6, approved in DECISIONS 1087). [CGEL](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/literature/huddlestonpullum2002.md:17097) says expressive attributives “make no contribution to the propositional meaning… but express the speaker's ill‑will, irritation…”; it nowhere states that *main* or *probable* lack stance. Coding N from taxonomy membership is classification by elimination, the same testimony move v4 elsewhere conceals. Keep these U in the primary; contrast‑N only as sensitivity (currently reversed).

Second, P07 in both diagnostic sets as primary. With P10/P11 thin, P07 is the diagnostic most likely to steer EXPRESSIVE (CONVERGENCE‑CHECK §Follow‑up, point 3), and it blurs INTJ's distribution/morphology anchor. Make no‑P07 EXPRESSIVE and no‑P07 INTJ co‑primary sensitivities at minimum; my preference is P07 out of INTJ's primary set entirely.

Third, admitting Tier B into v4 now, before Tier A yield is known, invites rescuing shallow targets with presences. The order (A, audit, B) is right; B's evidentiary status in the gate is not (I1).
