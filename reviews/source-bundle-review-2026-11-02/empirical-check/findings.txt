IMPORTANT QUALIFICATION — analysis/morphgain-confirmatory/RESULTS.md:69-71 says Brett completed a blind recode. The recode file contains 22 nonblank coder notes and secondary codes that differ from the primary record. These facts establish a distinct recorded coding pass and its internal attribution. They do not independently establish who made the judgments, whether original codes were concealed in practice, or how consistently the semantic criterion was applied. This audit did not inspect a signed provenance record or original interactive session and does not infer verified human independence from the filename, column label, or attribution alone.

If that recode's provenance and application of the rubric are sound, its 97 positive semantic assignments provide limited descriptive evidence of source-content continuity among the sampled retained derivatives. Undefined kappa is not evidence that those 97 observations are worthless; it means this chance-corrected agreement statistic is uninformative in the constant-margin case. The semantic criterion is source-associated content (impressed/evaluative response, disapproval/contempt, appreciative/surprised response, or away-sending), as defined at morphology-gain-coha-preregistration.md:104-121. It is not a test that the derivative itself contributes use-conditionally. The revision's distinction between content continuity and continued expressive-category membership is sound.

The sample cannot restore the claimed early-window confirmation: only 3 of its 97 rows belong to the 54 early-window rows — boo mgc0485 (1/17), ooh none (0/10), shoo mgc0575 (1/16), wow mgc0468 (1/11). It also cannot establish the coding accuracy of all 969 retained rows, the excluded population, or historical ordering merely by having 100% recorded semantic positives.

Suggested qualification to the revision: source lines 1077 ("Semantic persistence wasn't independently measured among the included derivatives"), 1098 ("they establish neither semantic persistence"), and 1118 ("absent independent semantic measurement") are too categorical unless explicitly scoped to the primary pipeline/full confirmatory dataset. Retain the withdrawal, but state that the archived recode records semantic residue for 97 retained tokens and may supply narrower descriptive evidence if its provenance is confirmed; it neither validates the original full-data coding nor evaluates the preregistered early windows adequately. No new analysis or new reliability claim should be silently substituted for the withdrawn one.

4. Fie: saved models, ordering, and a minor qualification

CONFIRMED — Model code at analysis/fie-confirmatory-model.R:102-145 fits three separate decade-binomial logistic regressions with an intercept N(0,1.5), slope N(0,0.5) restricted to negatives, four chains, 2,000 warmup and 2,000 later iterations, seed 2026. This follows the declared monotonic restriction; it cannot model a revival. Separate outcome likelihoods leave within-token diagnostic dependence unmodelled. The audit does not estimate what a joint model would produce.

CONFIRMED WITHOUT REFITTING — Reading analysis/fie-confirmatory-results.RData gives 8,000 saved tau draws for each outcome and an input total of 616. The saved and directly recalculated order probability are both 0.127875. Median and 95% interval values are syn 1946.640 [1937.189,1958.352], sem 1949.345 [1940.548,1960.214], int 1948.099 [1938.912,1958.510]. They round to the revised table. This validates faithful reporting of the saved numerical outputs, not their interpretation as linguistic erosion.

Saved RData SHA-256: 176f30eb69d724b9fc71d57f84ef8963249b994ad6ec249c912bb1c49df2cb6f.

CONFIRMED — For three iid continuous tau priors, exchangeability assigns each of the six strict orderings probability 1/6. The independent identical parameter priors induce identical continuous tau distributions. A 0.25 cutoff is therefore above that baseline; 0.13 alone is not strong evidence against the specific ordering. Overlapping marginal intervals cannot establish equality or simultaneity. The denominator failure independently blocks the advertised substantive interpretation.

MINOR QUALIFICATION — Revised source line 1160 notes decade labels versus preregistered decade midpoints. This is literally true, but centering removes a common midpoint offset from the predictor: (d+c)-mean(d+c) equals d-mean(d). Only conversion back to calendar year changes by c (roughly five years); the fitted likelihoods, relative ordering, and probability of ordering do not change for this reason. Avoid presenting it as an additional failure of the ordered-erosion model. It is a calendar-origin convention/error, unlike the denominator failure.
