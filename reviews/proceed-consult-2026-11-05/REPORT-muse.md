## 1. What Round 7 establishes, and what it does not

**Established, conditional on the simulated worlds.** INTJ membership is recoverable but imperfectly calibrated: full-record Brier skill 0.64 to 0.78 (audit-like), with posteriors of 0.4 to 0.6 corresponding to 0.30 to 0.44 true members (`cross/audit/q=0.55/split/intj/calibration`). EXPRESSIVE membership is barely recoverable: 0.20 to 0.28 audit-like, −0.02 to 0.16 under stress, and −0.12 [−0.58, 0.22] in the grade-of-membership world (`gom/audit/q=0.55/split/expr/recovery_full_bss`). This asymmetry is largely arithmetic. INTJ has six diagnostics at the assumed rates; EXPRESSIVE has four, of which P10S and P11N sit at 0.33/0.15, P11N is inapplicable on standalone rows, and P07 responds to either latent class ([harness.py](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/analysis/matrix-lesion/round7/harness.py:110)).

Only targets with large true gains are estimated usefully. INTJ P06 (D = 0.112, Type S 0.05) and P13 (D = 0.076, Type S 0.11) at audit 0.55, and even there estimate/D runs to 2.2 and 3.2 at the 95th percentile. Every other INTJ target has D ≈ 0.014 with Type S 0.54 to 0.65; every EXPRESSIVE target has D ≤ 0.047 with Type S 0.31 to 0.55. Signs there are coin flips. P12, the largest D (0.188), is computable in only 42% of datasets and recovers a median 0.08 of D. The shared-target INTJ − EXPRESSIVE mean has the right sign when computable (Type S 0.05 at audit 0.55), but it is computable in 42% of datasets, gated on P12 having both values, which is a selected subset, and it is attenuated (+0.038 against a true 0.071).

Record gain does not tell a cluster from bonds. Bonds family gains exceed cluster gains (+0.067 against +0.028 INTJ; `record_gain` AUC 0.03 to 0.21). One caveat [RESULTS.md](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/analysis/matrix-lesion/round7/RESULTS.md:106) omits: calibration matched only diagnostic–diagnostic phi ([RULE.md](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/analysis/matrix-lesion/round7/RULE.md:90)). Bonds targets draw from the same 40-bond pool as the diagnostics, and both fields share that pool, while world 1 makes the two classes independent given tier. Bonds therefore has diag–target and cross-field associations world 1 lacks by construction. The result proves gain alone is insufficient (Thomson's point), not that a target-matched diffuse alternative was fairly tested.

**RESULTS.md** is numerically accurate where I checked it (recovery table, checks, AUCs, P12 concealment median 33 all reproduce from the JSON). It misleads by emphasis in four places:

- Its §4 table shows the sufficiency-gap AUC at 0.82 to 0.92 for EXPRESSIVE, the one separator that works for either grouping, and never says so. It also never notes that cross-world sufficiency gaps are negative (medians −0.015 to −0.019), which under [FREEZE-v4.md](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/analysis/matrix-lesion/FREEZE-v4.md:131) cannot mean sufficiency. It means M3 loses to M2 through overfitting. In the independence world M1's family gain is −0.054 for INTJ, so every contrast involving M1 or M3 is confounded by estimator variance at roughly 25 reference rows.
- The PPC is described as having “no fixed reference” ([RESULTS.md](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/analysis/matrix-lesion/round7/RESULTS.md:184)). Stronger: as implemented it is invalid. It rejects the world that generated the data, and in the independence world, which is nested in the fitted model, its INTJ median runs 0.04, 0.51, 0.98 across the assumed rates. A valid check cannot do that.
- “The member class ranks true members above nonmembers in every world-1 dataset” ([RESULTS.md](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/analysis/matrix-lesion/round7/RESULTS.md:28)) is true (`oriented_share` = 1.0) and nearly empty. It coexists with skill at or below zero and with 0.6 to 0.8 posteriors that are 0.27 to 0.40 true members.
- Cap and split are called “similar” ([RESULTS.md](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/analysis/matrix-lesion/round7/RESULTS.md:164)) beside shared-mean Type S values of 0.15 and 0.05. Small n, but a threefold difference is not similarity.

**Not established.** Anything about the real record. Only P10S, P11N and P12 have measured rates, from 12 items predating the v4 rows; everything else runs on an assumed grid, an invented testimony process (three neat sources, inclusion `1.5z − .6` on true profiles, [harness.py](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/analysis/matrix-lesion/round7/harness.py:244)), and one cluster parameterization (rates 0.85/0.12). There are no direct property-to-property links of the kind the paper itself posits for prosody and syntax, except P07's either-cause form. The two-feature crosscutting model, three-class and no-P07 sensitivities, Tier B and absence-strength weights were not simulated ([RULE.md](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/analysis/matrix-lesion/round7/RULE.md:235)). ## 2. Ways to proceed

| Option | What the paper could claim | Cost | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. FREEZE-v4 as drafted, then production | Record reconstruction for a few INTJ targets; EXPRESSIVE and cross-inquiry most likely “unresolved” | Highest: 552-packet sealed audit, then the same coding again fresh | Very expensive near-null; bonds-like association misread as cluster evidence |
| B. Descriptive matrix, simple displays | Documented co-occurrence and per-target projection in the coded record, with counts; no cluster-versus-bonds claim | One open literature coding, reusable as a supplement | Weaker claim; the convergence score looks tally-like if promoted from display to assignment rule |
| C. Two-feature or grade-of-membership model, re-simulated first | Direct cross-inquiry target loadings | One more simulation round, then coding | More parameters at 46 rows; grade-of-membership recovery is already negative for EXPRESSIVE |
| D. Enrich or narrow first (Tier B, audio for P12, analyst tier, smaller inventory) | Whatever the enriched subset supports | Medium to high, property by property | Analyst judgments change the estimand from “the literature” to “the judges”; post hoc narrowing invites selection |
| E. No quantitative study; paper stands on its worked cases | Category pluralism argued case by case, as the abstract already does | Lowest | No quantitative projection test; a referee may ask for one |
| F. New primary data (judgments or audio corpus on held-out signs) | Genuine projective warrant to unseen cases | High; a different study | A different paper, with design and ethics overhead |

Option F appears nowhere in the project files. It is the only route to the projective-warrant claim in [FREEZE-v4.md](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/analysis/matrix-lesion/FREEZE-v4.md:68), since lesioning a frozen record can only ever deliver record reconstruction for documented cells.

## 3. Choice

Option B, shading into E. Stop FREEZE-v4 as a confirmatory lesion study. Code the 46 × 13 matrix once, openly, from Tier A quotations, and publish it as a descriptive supplement: per-target gains against base rates with counts, item-specific against inherited cells, member and nonmember contributions separated, no latent-class membership claims, no PPC, no sufficiency language. The simulation's clearest positive finding supports this: M2 does not beat the unweighted signed convergence score in the cluster world (INTJ median difference −0.002, positive in 49% of datasets; figure 7), and its margin over the anchor appears in the independence world too, where it is shrinkage toward M0, not convergence. The latent-class machinery buys nothing demonstrable, and [RULE.md](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/analysis/matrix-lesion/round7/RULE.md:19) already cites the relevant advice: simplify until it works. Keep the convergence score as a display, not an assignment rule, so your no-tally commitment is intact: what converged is shown cell by cell, and no row is declared a member by a score.

Evidence that would change my mind, any one of: (i) literature counts showing P08, P10S and P11N each known, with both polarities and substantial item-specific support, on well over half the EXPRESSIVE universe, since EXPRESSIVE failure is a diagnostic-supply failure; (ii) a re-simulation whose bonds world is also matched on diagnostic–target association, in which a predeclared statistic separates the worlds for both groupings; (iii) an audio source making P12 computable with both values as a matter of routine.

## 4. The two open decisions

**P11N.** Whole-column concealment should not stand. What leaks into P01 to P03 is the INAPPLICABLE state, not P11N's Y/N values on applicable rows. Conceal per cell, as [FREEZE-v4.md](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/analysis/matrix-lesion/FREEZE-v4.md:541) actually says: drop the inapplicability indicator for those three projections (the harness already zeroes it, [harness.py](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/queue/english-interjections/analysis/matrix-lesion/round7/harness.py:365)), keep applicable P11N cells visible, and count check (b)'s pairs over rows where joint visibility is possible. Under my choice in §3 the point is moot for gating, but it matters substantively either way: EXPRESSIVE projecting weakly onto distribution is itself evidence for inquiry-relativity, and the current construction makes those three projections unreportable instead of near-zero.

**Yield audit.** Do not run it as designed. A sealed, discarded 552-packet census is the most expensive possible way to obtain counts, and the simulation has narrowed what the counts could rescue: EXPRESSIVE needs P10S and P11N rates far above the 12-item audit's 4/12 and 2/12. The v4 expressive rows (Potts's and Ljung's items) might genuinely differ there. That is answerable with a small open check of P08, P10S and P11N on the expressive rows alone, at a fraction of the cost, and it doubles as the first slice of option B's coding. If you instead keep a confirmatory study alive, the sealed audit is the honest price of the firewall; I would not pay it on this evidence.
