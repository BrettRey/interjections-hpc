**Headline.** Neither grammar gives a form property that holds of two or more expressive uses and fails for the interjections and controls. LGSWE splits the expressive bearers by form:
- **Inserts.** DAMN_REACT, HEAVENS and GEE are expletives, which LGSWE treats as inserts. They occur standalone or at clause edges and can be linked prosodically to a host.
- **Normal clausal syntax.** BLOODY_PRENOM and FUCKING_PRENOM are explicitly "not themselves inserts" and occur "in the normal clausal syntax of English".
- **Exclamatory NPs.** YOU_BASTARD is excluded from the expletives as an exclamatory NP that combines "like vocatives" with a clause.
- **Formulae.** *damn you* is a non-imperative formula.

What LGSWE gives these uses in common is meaning only ("exclamatory", "emotive level"). In SIEG, "attributive-only" holds of DAMN_PRENOM but also of the control MAIN_PRENOM, which sits in the same list. Neither source says anything about insertion inside a word or phrase.

## 2. Form properties that hold of two or more expressive uses
- **Integrated modifier position (P03 Y).** Holds of DAMN_PRENOM, BLOODY_PRENOM, FUCKING_PRENOM and THE_HELL_WH, and equally of the controls MAIN_PRENOM, VERY_INTENS and BLACK. For the interjections it's hedged at class level ("in general … inability"); there's no N.
- **Standalone or clause-edge position (P01/P02 Y).** Holds of DAMN_REACT, HEAVENS, GEE (all expletives) and YOU_BASTARD (an exclamatory NP). It holds equally of every interjection and response form, so it separates inserts from integrated uses, not expressive from non-expressive. LGSWE excludes the prenominal and degree expressives from this class by name.
- **Attributive-only.** SIEG states it for DAMN_PRENOM, and for MAIN_PRENOM in the same list. Neither source says it about BLOODY_PRENOM or FUCKING_PRENOM. Neither says anything about it for PROBABLE_PRENOM or the interjections.
- **Dependents (P04 Y).** Holds of DAMN_NP (*damn you*) and the YOU_BASTARD class (*you little devil*), but also of THANKS, SORRY, *Hi there* and STOP.
- **Non-imperative form (*you*, not *yourself*).** LGSWE applies this test to DAMN_NP and to *thank you*, which links an expressive use to a routine formula.
- **Phonetic modification.** Holds of the moderated expletives (GEE; gosh, gawd) and equally of the response forms (yeah, yep, nope).
- **Insertion inside a word or phrase.** Nothing in either source. The nearest thing is a medial expletive inside a clause ("This is, God, a bloody afternoon wasted!"), which LGSWE calls "very rare".
- **Occurrence after wh-words.** THE_HELL_WH only. SIEG says the hell is "independent of clause type" (it also occurs in *Get the hell out*). Neither source covers EXACTLY_WH.

## 3. Warnings against using a property as a diagnostic
- LGSWE's insert criteria are tendencies ("rarely", "most common", "in general", "very rare") and are stated for "central members" of a class with a "gradual" boundary. They support Y values and notes, not N.
- LGSWE assigns uses of one form to different classes: bloody and fucking as adjectives or adverbs versus *bloody hell* as an expletive, and *What the hell* as a noun use. Its counts cover insert uses only. Forms shouldn't be pooled across uses.
- SIEG says attributive-only restrictions "often apply just to certain senses", and that gradability "applies to uses rather than lexemes".
- The controls share the candidate properties: MAIN is attributive-only, VERY has a host restriction and is repeated four times, BLACK and VERY are integrated.
- Repetition appears in non-expressive and interjection rows alike (oops oops, tt, bye bye, very ×4).
- P13 holds of response forms and hesitators, not just interjections.
- Inserts and expletives "can be linked prosodically" to a host (P12I Y). This sits against SIEG's account of supplements as separate intonation units.
- Functions overlap: oh and okay each serve several functions, and LGSWE says "different functions shade into one another" (1083).
- SIEG's silence on interjections is policy, so it never supports N.
- Classification disagrees across sources: LGSWE classes gee and heavens as moderated expletives, and you bastard and the dirty bugger as exclamatory NPs, not expletives.
