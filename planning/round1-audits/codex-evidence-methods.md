# Codex specialist: evidence and methods audit

## Overall verdict

The architecture contract's evidential ceiling is essentially right. The manuscript's strongest direct positive result is narrow: **selected overt verbal derivatives retain source-interjection semantics in their included VP-head uses**. Its best historical case is **Gehweiler's externally documented *Jesus!* to *gee!* pathway**. The *fie* analysis is a genuine preregistered failed prediction, though its reporting is incomplete. GloWbE establishes an overall country-section effect and strong regional concentration for some measured strings; it does not establish boundary decomposition, item-type-specific variety structure, or maintenance.

No present analysis demonstrates held-out forward projectibility, statistically reliable category-wide co-occurrence, a stable profile over time, or category-wide causal-network order.

## Revised empirical hierarchy

1. **Demonstrated conditional result:** semantic residue in selected included verbal derivatives.
2. **Failed prediction:** no support for ordered *fie* erosion.
3. **Supported external case:** parts of *Jesus!* to *gee!*.
4. **Supported descriptions:** tagged-inventory turnover; item-specific regional concentrations and category contrasts; overall GloWbE country-section variation.
5. **Reconstructive:** *damn*'s precise optative-to-interjection path and complement residue.
6. **Illustrative/prospective:** onomatopoeic and interactional recruitment generalizations, pathway-wide residue alignment, and the proposed causal coupling and maintenance claims.

Direct quotations pass the local quotation checker. The largest grounding failures are paraphrastic inferences, especially the false *gee* chronology and the *damn* tense-loss story.
